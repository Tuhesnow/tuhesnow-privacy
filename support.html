Frequently Asked Questions

What is TUHESNOW?

TUHESNOW is a productivity app designed to help you organize tasks, plan your day, build habits, track goals, and stay focused on what matters.

How do I create tasks and goals?

Open TUHESNOW and navigate to the Today or Goals section. Use the available creation controls to add new tasks or goals and personalize your plans.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

If you subscribed through Apple, open Settings on your iPhone or iPad, tap your Apple Account, select Subscriptions, and choose TUHESNOW. Subscription management is handled by Apple.

What if my premium purchase is not recognized?

Make sure you are using the Apple Account associated with your purchase. If the issue continues, contact our support team for help. Never share your Apple Account password.

How can I report a technical problem?

Email our support team with a description of the issue, your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots may help, but please avoid sending passwords or sensitive personal information.

How can I contact TUHESNOW?

Send an email to TuhesnowHelp@outlook.com .
