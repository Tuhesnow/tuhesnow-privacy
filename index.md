## 4. Information Processed by TUHESNOW

Depending on the features you use, TUHESNOW processes tasks and recurring tasks, titles and notes, goals and daily check-ins, completion history and streaks, calendar events, planner information, focus sessions and history, progress information, app preferences, personalization, user-selected images, personal notification sounds, import/export data and subscription status.

Your primary productivity information is stored locally. TUHESNOW does not require a TUHESNOW account or operate its own central account database. Apple and the providers described below process information for their services, including SDK-generated technical identifiers.

If you contact us, we and the email or communications providers involved process the contact details and information you choose to send to handle your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive content.

## 5. Local Storage

Primary app data and local recovery copies are stored in the app's protected storage. Preferences use platform storage such as UserDefaults. TUHESNOW also keeps local product-diagnostic counters without task or goal contents or user identifiers. These counters continue when remote analytics are off and are not uploaded as historical events.

The app stores one analytics-consent state. Permission to use remote analytics includes your confirmation that you are 18 or older. No date of birth, birth year or separate age profile is stored. Declining does not record whether you are under 18 or simply prefer not to share analytics.

RevenueCat and, after opt-in, TelemetryDeck maintain their own local SDK information and caches. Deleting app content or resetting it does not necessarily clear preferences, SDK caches, system backups or separately exported files.

## 6. iCloud and CloudKit

iCloud synchronization is optional. When enabled, supported productivity data, history, personalization and personal media are synchronized through the private CloudKit database of the Apple Account signed into iCloud. TUHESNOW does not run a separate server that stores your private CloudKit content.

A locally stored one-way fingerprint of the CloudKit account identifier helps detect account changes; TUHESNOW does not send that fingerprint to RevenueCat or TelemetryDeck. When the account changes, the app asks you to connect the new account before uploading.

Turning sync off stops new TUHESNOW sync operations but does not erase an existing iCloud copy. Deletions and reset information are synchronized when sync is enabled, access is available and synchronization succeeds. Offline changes and media cleanup may remain pending until a later successful sync. You can also manage iCloud storage in Apple's settings.

Apple's terms, privacy practices and account security settings apply. This policy does not promise that all CloudKit data is end-to-end encrypted.

## 7. Calendar Integration

Calendar access is optional. TUHESNOW requests permission when you choose calendar functionality. For import, you enable the integration and select eligible editable personal calendars. After that choice, the app can refresh imported events automatically while the integration remains enabled and permission is available.

The import copies titles, start/end dates and times, all-day information and local EventKit references needed to maintain the connection. It does not import event notes, attendees or locations. Turning the integration off removes its imported event copies from TUHESNOW; it does not delete the original calendar events.

A separate user-requested export can write TUHESNOW event titles, dates, times and notes to a writable calendar, and a recurrence rule for birthdays. Calendar providers configured on your device may then synchronize those exported copies under their own practices. Disabling import alone does not remove exported calendar copies. Deletion attempts can require calendar permission; remaining copies may need manual removal.

You can change the integration in TUHESNOW and permissions in system settings.

## 8. Photos, Images, Audio and Personal Media

Personal media is optional. For normal photo selection, Apple's system picker lets you choose specific items without granting unrestricted access to your library. Microphone permission is requested when you choose to record a sound; audio files can also be imported through user-selected file workflows.

The normal background-image processing path renders a new JPEG without copying source EXIF, GPS or IPTC metadata. This is not a guarantee for every imported file: backup, transfer and other file-based workflows can retain metadata already present in the files.

Personal media is stored locally and, where supported and enabled, in private CloudKit storage. Media exports follow the categories you select. Temporary audio drafts and notification-delivery copies may also be created; the app cleans drafts and media through its existing cleanup mechanisms.

## 9. Notifications

With notification permission, TUHESNOW schedules local reminders. Depending on the feature and your selections, they can contain task or event titles, event notes, dates, times, images and selected sounds. Visibility follows your device's notification-preview and lock-screen settings.

A local delivery copy allows a selected notification sound to play while the device is locked after its first unlock. TUHESNOW does not operate a remote reminder server. CloudKit can use Apple Push Notification service for background synchronization notifications; that is separate from ordinary local reminders.

## 10. Widgets

Widgets read a limited derived cache shared locally through an Apple App Group. It can contain task, goal and event titles, neutral local routing identifiers, access-capability flags, completion and focus information, dates, times, durations, progress and streaks.

The cache does not contain task notes, personal image/audio contents, RevenueCat account information, TelemetryDeck identifiers or CloudKit account identifiers. TUHESNOW does not upload the widget cache to a TUHESNOW-operated server. Widget actions use the app's existing operations; the same optional sync and analytics choices apply to the resulting app activity.

Widget contents may be visible to anyone who can see your Home Screen. You choose whether to add widgets. An old display may remain until the system refreshes it.

## 11. Purchases and Subscriptions

Auto-renewable subscriptions are purchased through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment-card details; TUHESNOW does not receive or store them.

TUHESNOW uses RevenueCat, Inc. for purchase validation, subscription status, entitlements and subscription reporting. The SDK is configured when the app's subscription service starts, rather than only after a purchase. This processing is independent of the optional TelemetryDeck setting and its 18+ confirmation.

RevenueCat receives a generated anonymous App User ID and may process receipts or transaction information, product and transaction identifiers, purchase/renewal/expiration/refund status, timestamps, app/build and operating-system information, device and runtime information, storefront, preferred locales and related request metadata. The installed Apple SDK sends the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), when available, in request metadata. Network requests also expose connection information such as the source IP address to the receiving infrastructure.

“Anonymous App User ID” is RevenueCat's term for a generated identifier; it does not mean that no technical identifiers or transaction records are processed. TUHESNOW supplies no custom account ID, contact Customer Attributes, advertising ID or iCloud identifier. No RevenueCat webhooks or external integrations are currently enabled. RevenueCat's dashboard can provide subscription statistics in addition to entitlement management. TUHESNOW does not use RevenueCat for advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

## 12. Optional Usage Analytics

TUHESNOW uses TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany, for optional product analytics. The remote provider is not initialized unless you both confirm “I am 18 or older” and separately allow analytics. Old permission without that confirmation does not enable it. Declining or withdrawing does not reduce app features or subscription access.

You can withdraw in Settings or through the Usage analytics choice available when onboarding or the subscription screen hides Settings. Removing the 18+ confirmation also withdraws permission. The app stops forwarding events and disables/cancels its SDK transport. Requests already transmitted cannot be recalled.

TUHESNOW forwards only these product-event names:
- app_opened
- onboarding_started
- onboarding_completed
- paywall_viewed
- task_created
- task_completed
- focus_started
- focus_completed
- planner_opened
- goal_created

The app adds no task, goal or calendar-event names, notes, content identifiers, email addresses, subscription tiers or purchase details as event parameters, and supplies no custom user ID. “goal_created”, for example, is a technical event type, not the title of your goal.

The SDK adds platform-dependent technical metadata, including app/OS versions, device and screen information, language and regional settings, accessibility/display settings, session and retention information, timestamps, a session identifier and a hashed device-related identifier. It can derive its default identifier from IDFV; TUHESNOW does not link it to your name, email or Apple Account. TelemetryDeck applies further server-side anonymization according to its documentation. We do not equate hashing alone with a guarantee of legal anonymity.

The SDK can queue signals locally. Withdrawal and app-content reset do not purge that queue. A later opt-in or re-enable may transmit older queued signals, including signals from an earlier app version. This is separate from the app's local counters, which are not replayed. TUHESNOW does not use TelemetryDeck for advertising, IDFA-based tracking, cross-app advertising tracking or data-broker activities.

## 13. Analytics Data Retention

TelemetryDeck distinguishes the period during which data is queryable under a plan from longer storage. Its published FAQ says older events are kept in cold storage and gives no guaranteed automatic deletion schedule. Expiry of dashboard access therefore does not mean deletion.

Local SDK queues can also persist; TUHESNOW does not promise a fixed queue-deletion period. Withdrawal stops new forwarding/transmissions through the app's configured provider but does not delete information already received by TelemetryDeck.

Contact us with privacy requests. We will assess and respond under applicable law. Because we do not link analytics identifiers to your contact details, we may be unable to identify particular analytics records from your name or email alone. We will explain relevant limitations rather than promise individual deletion that cannot be verified.

## 14. Imports, Exports and Backups

Import and export are user-initiated. The app can also maintain automatic local recovery copies, which are separate from the export you request. Exported backups and System/Plan packages contain the selected supported categories; selected media and Apple Calendar imports can contain personal information.

Files saved outside TUHESNOW may be held locally, in iCloud Drive or by another Files provider you select. Those copies are controlled by their holder and storage provider. Resetting or uninstalling TUHESNOW does not delete them. Store and share exports securely.

## 15. Legal Bases for Processing

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, processing necessary to provide the app, subscriptions and features you request is based on performance of the contract (Article 6(1)(b)). Optional remote TelemetryDeck analytics are based on consent (Article 6(1)(a)); the app additionally requires an 18+ confirmation.

Processing needed to meet a specific legal obligation is based on Article 6(1)(c). Where necessary and subject to the required balancing of interests, security, fraud prevention, operational subscription reporting, handling enquiries and legal claims can rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)). Those interests are operating and protecting TUHESNOW, administering subscriptions and responding to requests and claims.

Calendar, microphone, notifications and media selection also depend on the relevant platform permissions and user choices. A system permission is separate from consent to remote analytics. If information needed for a requested feature is unavailable, that feature may not operate; refusing analytics has no such effect.

## 18. Data Retention and Deletion

Local productivity data is retained for your use until you delete it or reset the app, subject to recovery copies, pending operations and system backups. Removing the app does not erase independently stored iCloud data, exports or provider records.

Private CloudKit copies can remain until a supported deletion/reset synchronizes successfully or you manage the storage through Apple. Disabling sync alone does not delete them. Reset clears the app's widget cache, but WidgetKit controls when an already rendered widget refreshes.

RevenueCat retains subscription and transaction information under its service terms and applicable operational and legal requirements; this policy does not promise an unverified fixed period. TelemetryDeck's active query period is distinct from cold storage as explained above. Apple controls its own payment and transaction records under its terms and legal obligations. Exported files remain where you saved or shared them until removed there.

## 19. Reset and Deletion Requests

TUHESNOW provides individual deletion and Delete all app data. A reset clears the app's productivity content, local diagnostic counters and widget cache through the existing reset process. It does not reset analytics consent, cancel a subscription, delete SDK identity/cache data, erase separately exported files or necessarily remove external calendar copies, all system backups or provider-held records.

For privacy rights relating to information held by providers, contact TuhesnowHelp@outlook.com. We will handle the request under applicable law and involve the relevant provider where needed. We may need proportionate information to locate records or verify a request; we do not promise to identify an anonymous analytics record from an email address. Statutory rights and response obligations remain applicable.

## 21. Children and Young Users

TUHESNOW is a general-audience productivity app. The app's analytics-age choice does not exclude people under 18 from onboarding, features or subscription functionality. Apple's parental controls, purchase/account rules and applicable law still apply.

Remote TelemetryDeck analytics require a confirmation of being at least 18 followed by a separate voluntary opt-in. No birth date or separate age profile is requested or stored. Unconfirmed, declined and withdrawn states do not initialize the remote provider on launch. This is not an age gate for the app or a parental-consent system. RevenueCat subscription processing is separate, as described above.

## 22. Advertising and Tracking

TUHESNOW contains no third-party advertising network and does not use IDFA, data-broker activities or cross-context behavioral advertising. No App Tracking Transparency prompt is used because the current app does not perform advertising tracking across other companies' apps or websites. Technical identifiers and subscription/analytics processing still occur as described above; absence of an ATT prompt does not mean absence of all data processing.

## 23. Security

TUHESNOW uses Apple sandboxing, platform file-protection mechanisms, restricted widget projections and encrypted service connections. Protection varies by storage type: for example, a reminder sound delivery copy remains available after first unlock. No method guarantees absolute security or end-to-end encryption for all data. We will assess and handle personal-data incidents under applicable legal requirements.
